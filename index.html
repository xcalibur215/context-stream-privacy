Context Stream privacy policy

Effective 18 November 2026.

Context Stream is a browser extension that saves selected AI conversations locally and helps you review text to carry into another chat.

Information handled

When you enable a supported site and choose to save a conversation, the extension reads visible conversation text and related information on that page, such as conversation URLs, titles, message structure, and references to attachments. Conversations may contain personal or sensitive information that you or the AI included. The extension may retain revisions, handoff records, and settings. It can temporarily store opening-turn data in browser session storage to link a reviewed handoff.

Storage and sharing

Saved conversations are stored in the extension's local browser storage and IndexedDB. Context Stream does not operate a backend, require a Context Stream account, or send saved conversations to the developer. Site access is optional and requested separately for each supported provider.

When you choose Copy or Insert, the reviewed text is copied to the clipboard or placed in the destination site's composer. If you send it, the destination AI provider receives it under that provider's own policies. The extension does not press Send for you. Exported backups and handoff files are saved on your device and may contain unencrypted conversation text. You control where you store or share them.

Your choices and retention

You can disable access to connected sites, delete saved conversations in the extension, and export or import backups. Local records remain in your browser profile until you delete them or remove the extension or profile. Removing the extension or losing the browser profile can erase its local data. Exported files and copies you send to other services must be managed separately.

Limitations

The extension reads rendered content, which may omit unloaded history or media. Review handoffs and warnings before sharing, and keep separate backups of important information.
